Long for press and speaker profiles

Maxwell Cofie is fascinated by what technology can do for people, and nowhere more than across Africa. Based in Accra, Ghana, he works across three connected areas: product, capital and community.

Through Stage 2, an Accra-based product studio, he has spent more than eight years building digital products and launching ventures across fintech, legal-tech, events, commerce and proptech. Stage 2's products include Vellum, for wills and digital legacy planning; GrayDocket, for business registration and compliance; GatePass, for event discovery and ticketing; and Motor Ambos, for car papers and roadside help.

Through The Good Seed Capital, he bridges the gap between early-stage African founders and global investors who back with conviction, not curiosity. Through Sandbox Réseau, a gathering in Accra for Africa's founders and builders, and The Reverb, a podcast of short, honest conversations, he helps builders learn from one another.

He writes about building, funding and connecting in Africa's tech ecosystem in The Logbook at maxwellcofie.com.
