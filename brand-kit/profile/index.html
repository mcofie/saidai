Maxwell Cofie

Founder of Stage 2, The Good Seed Capital and Sandbox Réseau

Accra, Ghana · Say it: MAX-well KOH-fee

Maxwell Cofie is an Accra-based founder working across product, capital and community in Africa's tech ecosystem. Through Stage 2, the product studio he founded, he has spent more than eight years building digital products and launching ventures across fintech, legal-tech, events, commerce and proptech in Ghana. He also founded The Good Seed Capital, which connects early-stage African founders with global investors, and Sandbox Réseau, a builder community in Accra and home of The Reverb podcast.

Sandbox Réseau · Builder community

A gathering in Accra for Africa's founders and builders, with demos, mini-talks and open networking. Home of The Reverb, a five-minute podcast of builders' hard-earned lessons.
